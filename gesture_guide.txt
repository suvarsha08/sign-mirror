================================================================
  SIGNMIRROR – Gesture Quick-Reference Guide
================================================================

Below is how to perform each of the five supported gestures
in front of the camera.  Hold each pose steadily for about
half a second so the stability buffer can confirm the sign.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. HELLO  (Wave / Open Palm)
   • Hold your hand up, palm facing the camera.
   • Extend ALL five fingers wide apart (spread them).
   • Keep your hand still (or wave gently).

2. YES  (Thumbs Up)
   • Make a closed fist.
   • Extend ONLY your thumb straight up.
   • Keep the other four fingers curled in.

3. NO  (Index Finger Only)
   • Make a closed fist.
   • Extend ONLY your index finger upward.
   • Keep thumb, middle, ring, and pinky closed.

4. OK  (OK Sign / Circle)
   • Touch your thumb tip to your index finger tip,
     forming a small circle / ring.
   • Extend your middle, ring, and pinky fingers upward.

5. THANK YOU  (Flat Palm / Fingers Together)
   • Hold your hand up with ALL fingers extended.
   • Keep your fingers TOGETHER (not spread apart).
   • Palm can face the camera or be angled slightly.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TIPS
  • Face a well-lit area; avoid strong back-lighting.
  • Keep your hand within the camera frame, ideally
    at chest-to-face height.
  • Hold each gesture steadily – quick flicks may be
    filtered out by the stability buffer.
  • You can use either hand; the system detects both.
